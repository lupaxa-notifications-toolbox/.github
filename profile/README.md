<h1 align="center">The Lupaxa Notifications Toolbox</h1>

**The Lupaxa Notifications Toolbox** contains tools, libraries and utilities for notifications, messaging and alerting.

The organisation focuses on providing reliable, well-documented and reusable software that simplifies sending notifications, integrating messaging platforms
and automating alerts across a wide range of services and communication channels.

## Focus

**The Lupaxa Notifications Toolbox** contains tools for areas including:

- Notification delivery
- Messaging platform integration
- Alerting and incident notifications
- SMS and mobile messaging
- Email notifications
- ChatOps and collaboration platform integration
- Notification automation and workflows
- Developer and operational messaging utilities

Supported services may include Textlocal, Pushover, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Telegram, email providers and many other notification and messaging platforms.

Whether you're sending operational alerts, integrating messaging services into your applications, automating notifications or building communication workflows,
**The Lupaxa Notifications Toolbox** aims to provide practical, reusable tools that simplify delivering notifications across multiple platforms.
